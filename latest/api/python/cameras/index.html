Cameras and sensor models

Camera models read from mission files, and instrument presets.

CahvoreModel

A camera in JPL's CAHV, CAHVOR or CAHVORE model, as Mars rovers' cameras are calibrated. Image coordinates are the models': (0, 0) the centre of the upper-left pixel, x right, y down.

Methods:

Name Description
author_camera

Authors the Camera prim at path (a str, Path, Prim or typed prim) as the model in Ernie's camera (perspective with Brown-Conrady terms, or a Kannala-Brandt fisheye for CAHVORE), posed in the model's frame, and with rays_step above 0 the model's own rays every that many pixels (ernie:lens:rays), which the sensor follows in place of the fit: exact but for CAHVORE's moving entrance pupil. Returns (width, height, rows_reversed, residual_pixels), the residual the fit's alone: render at that resolution and flip the rows if rows_reversed.
fit

How the model converts to Ernie's camera at pixel pitch pixel_pitch_um: its residual_pixels, pupil_shift_max, field_angle_max (radians), focal_length_mm and whether it is a fisheye.

author_lroc_nac 

author_lroc_nac(
    scene: Scene,
    camera: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    product: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    side: str = "left",
) -> None

Authors one of LROC's Narrow Angle Cameras (NAC-L or NAC-R) as Robinson et al. 2010 publish it (Table 2, Appendix A): Camera camera's sensor (400-760 nm, gain, detector noise, full well, 12-bit ADC, f-number) and RenderProduct product's readout chain (qe, noise, adc, even/odd readoutTaps, compandTable code 0) whose RawDN is the 8-bit numbers the NAC sends. Its geometry comes from the image's ISD (IsdLineScanner.author_strip). camera and product are each a str, Path, Prim or typed prim.

author_lroc_wac 

author_lroc_wac(
    scene: Scene,
    camera: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    product: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    band: int = 605,
) -> None

Authors one band of LROC's Wide Angle Camera (320, 360, 415, 565, 605, 645 or 690 nm, by name or effective wavelength) as Robinson et al. 2010 publish it (Tables 3 and 4): Camera camera's sensor (the passband at the effective QE that gives Table 4's responsivity, gain, detector noise, full well, 11-bit ADC, f-number; its spectral range narrowed to the passband) and RenderProduct product's readout chain (qe, noise, adc). Its geometry comes from the image's ISD (IsdPushFrame.author_framelet). camera and product are each a str, Path, Prim or typed prim.

author_mars2020_navcam 

author_mars2020_navcam(
    scene: Scene,
    camera: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    product: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    pattern: str = "rggb",
    jpeg_quality: float = 85.0,
) -> None

Authors one of the Mars 2020 rover's Navcams as Maki et al. 2020 publish it (Table 2): Camera camera's f-theta fisheye (19.1 mm, f/12, 96 x 73 degrees over 5120 x 3840 pixels of 6.4 um) and RGB sensor (gain 0.24 DN/e-, 8 e- dark noise, 15,000 e- full well, 12-bit ADC), and RenderProduct product's chain (qe, bayer, noise, adc, demosaic; cie, exposure, jpeg). The paper gives neither the Bayer layout (pattern) nor the JPEG quality (jpeg_quality). camera and product are each a str, Path, Prim or typed prim.

author_mastcamz(

author_singlet_chromatic 

author_singlet_chromatic(
    scene: Scene,
    camera: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    nd: float = 1.5168,
    abbe: float = 64.17,
    lateral: float = 0.0,
) -> None

Authors Camera camera's chromatic aberration (ernie:lens:chromatic:scale and :focus) as a thin singlet of its focal length in a glass of index nd and Abbe number abbe (by default SCHOTT N-BK7) would have: axial colour from the glass's dispersion (Cauchy, f_C - f_F = f / V), lateral colour lateral times its relative focal change (0 with the stop at the lens, up to 1 for a distant stop). The defocus filter blurs by the axial colour; the lens resamples each band by the lateral. camera is a str, Path, Prim or typed prim.
