Cameras and sensor models

Camera models read from mission files, planetary elevation models, and lens helpers. Instruments built from them as published, such as LROC's cameras and the Mars 2020 rover's, are demos: demos/cameras/.

CahvoreModel

A camera in JPL's CAHV, CAHVOR or CAHVORE model, as Mars rovers' cameras are calibrated. Image coordinates are the models': (0, 0) the centre of the upper-left pixel, x right, y down.

Methods:

Name Description
author_camera

Authors the Camera prim at path (a str, Path, Prim or typed prim) as the model in Ernie's camera (perspective with Brown-Conrady terms, or a Kannala-Brandt fisheye for CAHVORE), posed in the model's frame, and with rays_step above 0 the model's own rays every that many pixels (ernie:lens:rays), which the sensor follows in place of the fit: exact but for CAHVORE's moving entrance pupil. Returns (width, height, rows_reversed, residual_pixels), the residual the fit's alone: render at that resolution and flip the rows if rows_reversed.

local_terrain_of 

local_terrain_of(
    dem: PolarStereographicDem | CylindricalDem,
    latitude: float,
    longitude: float,
    cells: int,
    cell_size: float,
) -> dict

The cells x cells heightfield of dem, cell_size m apart, about a latitude and east longitude (degrees), in a local frame there (x east, y north, z up, its origin on the reference sphere), the planet's curvature in the heights: a dict of heights (float32, the first row northern), origin (x, y of the north-western cell), cell_size and body_to_local (body-fixed metres to the local frame).

author_singlet_chromatic 

author_singlet_chromatic(
    scene: Scene,
    camera: str | Path | Prim | Schema,
    nd: float = 1.5168,
    abbe: float = 64.17,
    lateral: float = 0.0,
) -> None

Authors Camera camera's chromatic aberration (ernie:lens:chromatic:scale and :focus) as a thin singlet of its focal length in a glass of index nd and Abbe number abbe (by default SCHOTT N-BK7) would have: axial colour from the glass's dispersion (Cauchy, f_C - f_F = f / V), lateral colour lateral times its relative focal change (0 with the stop at the lens, up to 1 for a distant stop). The defocus filter blurs by the axial colour; the lens resamples each band by the lateral. camera is a str, Path, Prim or typed prim.
