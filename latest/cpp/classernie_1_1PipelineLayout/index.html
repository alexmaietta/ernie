Detailed Description

Every pass binds its per-dispatch resources (render targets, storage images, a uniform block) by pushing descriptors when it records; its long-lived textures are heap indices and its buffers addresses (GpuBuffer::Address), so no pass needs descriptor pools of its own, and no more than a few push bindings.
