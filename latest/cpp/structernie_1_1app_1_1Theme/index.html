Struct ernie::app::Theme

ClassList > ernie > app > Theme

Colours (sRGB-encoded with alpha, as written to the screen) and metrics of the viewer's UI: a classic scientific desktop look, as Qt's and GTK's of the 2000s that tools such as PANGU wear (colours read from PANGU v7's renderer GUI): light grey-green windows (#D4D7D0), white sunken fields and raised buttons with one-pixel edges, square corners, black text, a navy accent and pale-blue selection.

  • #include <ernie/app/theme.h>
