Detailed Description

The defaults follow NVIDIA Omniverse Kit's dark look: a near-black tab bar and docking area, charcoal panels, darker input fields, grey labels with light values, section headers as lighter bars, a steel-blue accent for selection and changed values, and dark translucent pills over the viewport.
