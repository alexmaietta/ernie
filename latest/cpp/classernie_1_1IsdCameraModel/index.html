Detailed Description

  • Image coordinates are CSM's: (line, sample) with the first pixel's centre at (0.5, 0.5).
  • The sensor's position is sampled as usgscsm samples it: at least 25 even times over the states, each a cubic Hermite of the states around it (ALE's spline) rotated into the body's frame, then interpolated eighth-order Lagrange (fewer points near the ends). Its orientation, sensor to body = body rotation x inverse pointing, is taken at the union of their times (each interpolated along the great arc), resampled evenly and interpolated the same way.
  • Detector line d and sample s lie on the focal plane at the inverse of focal2pixel of (d x line summing + starting detector line - detector centre line, s x sample summing + starting detector sample - detector centre sample), in mm; the lens's distortion moves it; the camera looks along (-x, -y, -f) in the sensor's frame.

Light-time and aberration corrections are left out, as usgscsm leaves them out.

  • std::invalid_argument if isd has no states or rotations, a singular focal plane map, or a distortion model other than none and usgscsm's: "radial", "transverse", "radtan" (OpenCV's radial-tangential, coefficients k1, k2, p1, p2, k3 on coordinates over the focal length), "lrolrocnac", "kaguyalism", "dawnfc", "cahvor", "kplo_shadowcam" and "cassis" (TGO CaSSIS's rational model, its 18 correcting coefficients then 18 distorting); or with the wrong number of coefficients for its model.
