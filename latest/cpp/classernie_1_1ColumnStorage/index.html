Class ernie::ColumnStorage

ClassList > ernie > ColumnStorage

Storage a consumer of columns offers for them on a device it reads them on, such as a renderer's interop buffers: a column mapped there ( Column::Map ) is then written where the consumer reads it, copying nothing.More...
