Struct ernie::IsdRotation

ClassList > ernie > IsdRotation

A rotation sampled over time between two NAIF frames, as ALE reads it ( ale::Orientations ): quaternions (w, x, y, z, scalar first) of the time-dependent rotation of vectors from the reference frame into the first oftimeDependentFrames , and a constant rotation (row-major, applied to vectors after it) on into the first ofconstantFrames .
