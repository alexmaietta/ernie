Class ernie::Overlay

ClassList > ernie > Overlay

A named layer of debug drawing: lines, shapes, markers and labels in world coordinates, drawn over the images of render products that ask for it ( tokens::kOverlays ), after the sensor filters, so they never reach observations that do not.More...

Detailed Description

Items stay until Clear; a simulation redrawing each step clears and adds again.

Every shape reduces to segments, triangles and sprites (Segments, Triangles, Sprites), which the renderer draws as they are.
