struct FrameView
A frame of the detector as Ernie renders it: a frame camera of the image's samples by its rows, posed ( camera.transform , camera to body-fixed world) when the frame is exposed, the lens's distortion fitted as Brown-Conrady terms (and scale).
class IsdFrame
A frame camera from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's frame model ( UsgsAstroFrameSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): the whole image is exposed at once, at the ISD's centre time, from one position and orientation.
class IsdLineScanner
A line scanner (pushbroom camera) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's line scanner model ( UsgsAstroLsSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): line l is exposed around its timeImageTime(l) , a rate's start time plus its seconds per line times (l - its start line + 0.5), through detector line 0.
struct Strip
A strip of lines image lines fromfirstLine as Ernie renders it: a pushbroom camera (CameraParams::pushbroom ) whose rows are those lines, posedfrom at the strip's first line's start andto at its end (camera to body-fixed world).
class IsdPushFrame
A push-frame camera (LROC's WAC, MRO's MARCI) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's push-frame model ( UsgsAstroPushFrameSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): the image is a stack of framelets, each a frame ofFrameletLines lines exposed at once.
struct Framelet
Framelet __framelet (in image order, from the top) as Ernie renders it: a frame camera of the image's samples byFrameletLines lines, posed when it is exposed (camera to body-fixed world), its exposure the ISD's, the lens's distortion fitted as Brown-Conrady terms (and scale).
struct IsdRotation
A rotation sampled over time between two NAIF frames, as ALE reads it ( ale::Orientations ): quaternions (w, x, y, z, scalar first) of the time-dependent rotation of vectors from the reference frame into the first oftimeDependentFrames , and a constant rotation (row-major, applied to vectors after it) on into the first ofconstantFrames .
struct IsdSamples <typename T>
States sampled over time: ephemeris times (seconds past J2000, TDB) and a value at each.
struct IsdSensor
A camera's image support data (ISD) in the Community Sensor Model's JSON form, as ALE (the USGS Abstraction Layer for Ephemerides) writes it from SPICE kernels for planetary cameras such as LRO's NAC and WAC, MRO's CTX and HiRISE: the image's size and timing, the focal plane's map to detector pixels, the optics, and the spacecraft's and the body's states over the image.
