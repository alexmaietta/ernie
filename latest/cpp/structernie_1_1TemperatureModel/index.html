Detailed Description

A material names one in ernie:thermal:model; its pixels take the model's temperature in place of the material's own, though a mesh's solver temperatures, a prim's and a temperature texture still win.

slang is a Slang module that may import temperature_model; (TemperatureModelSlang) and defines a struct named typeName implementing ITemperatureModel, whose gives the temperature at a pixel from input: its point and outward surface normal (world, relative to the frame's origin); every distant light linked to it (lightCount of them, at most kTemperatureLights, none singled out): its direction, its irradiance over all wavelengths facing it (W/m^2) and how much of it reaches the point past shadows; the sunlight on the surface from them all (W/m^2, shadowed); the pixel's solar absorptivity (the material's, scaled as its albedo textures scale its reflectance) and the material's thermal emissivity; its own temperature (0 for none); the frame's time (s); the world +z axis of a prim bound to the material (0 for none); and the pixel's thermal inertia (its thermal inertia texture's, else the material's; 0 for none). Parameter i is p.get(i), the material's ernie:thermal:model:data p.data(i) (p.dataCount() of them).
