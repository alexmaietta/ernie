The density of a gas in hydrostatic equilibrium under the temperature profile of the U.S. 

static DensityProfile ernie::DensityProfile::StandardAtmosphere (
    double surfaceTemperature,
    double molarMass,
    double gravity
)

Standard Atmosphere 1976: its lapse rates from the ground (-6.5 K/km to 11 km, 0 to 20 km, +1 to 32 km, +2.8 to 47 km, 0 to 51 km, -2.8 to 71 km, -2 to 84.852 km, then constant), shifted to start at surfaceTemperature, relative to the ground's: d ln p / dh = -M g / (R T) and density p / T, tabulated every 100 m to 120 km. Heights are taken as geometric (the standard's are geopotential, 1.6% apart at 100 km).

References: * NOAA, NASA and USAF, "U.S. Standard Atmosphere, 1976", NOAA-S/T 76-1562, 1976, table 4. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19770009539
