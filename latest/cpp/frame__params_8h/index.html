Per-frame parameters of a render, whatever the backend: the camera, the sun, the other lights, the sensor filters and the frame's place in the random streams.

A light besides the sun, resolved for a frame from its prim ( LightParamsOf ).
struct Beam
A laser's Gaussian beam (Siegman 1986): its 1/e^2 radius at the waist along the light's x and y, its Rayleigh range along each, and the waist's distance along the beam from position , m.
struct PushbroomStrip
A pushbroom camera's strip for one frame ( CameraParams::pushbroom ), as its shaders project with it (projection_pushbroom.slang): its rows' poses run fromfrom (the top row's camera position) toto (the bottom row's), turning fromfromRotation totoRotation along the great arc.
struct ShadowSettings
Cascaded shadow map options.
struct Star
One star of a StarField , as renders take it: where it is and the black-body spectrum it shines with.

The stars of a StarField prim (StarsOf ) are uploaded when they change; each frame takes only where the catalogue's frame lies (StarFieldParamsOf ).
