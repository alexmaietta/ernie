Detailed Description

Lit faces of any cloud, taken locally as such slabs, read their multiple scattering from it.

References: * A. Bouthors, F. Neyret, N. Max, E. Bruneton and C. Crassin, "Interactive multiple anisotropic scattering in clouds", ACM SIGGRAPH Symposium on Interactive 3D Graphics and Games 2008 (the canonical transport function and its collectors, by Monte Carlo in slabs; stored here as a lattice rather than fitted). * S. Chandrasekhar, "Radiative Transfer", Dover 1960 (plane-parallel slabs).
