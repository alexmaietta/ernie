Detailed Description

Shaders keep their samples to bound tiles by sampling no finer than the finest mip resident there (VirtualTexture's min-LOD map).

Tiles have the format's standard block shape (64 KiB each: 128 x 128 texels of 32-bit texels, 256 x 256 of BC7). Mips smaller than a tile share the mip tail, bound at creation and written with WriteTail. Binding waits for the bind alone; texels are copied in submissions the host does not wait for, ordered before work submitted after them. Releasing unbinds at once: callers keep work in flight off the tiles they release (VirtualTexture lets a frame pass first).

Requires VulkanContext::SupportsSparseResidency.

Reference: Khronos, "Sparse Resources", Vulkan specification, chapter 33. https://docs.vulkan.org/spec/latest/chapters/sparsemem.html
