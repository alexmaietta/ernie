Struct ernie::LinkGroups

ClassList > ernie > LinkGroups

Geometry grouped by the lights that light it and whose shadows it casts, as the rasterizer applies links: each surface carries its group (0, lit and shadowed by every light, for prims no link singles out), and each light a mask of the groups it lights and of those that cast its shadows. More...
