Class ernie::BlockEncoder

ClassList > ernie > BlockEncoder

Encodes RGBA8 images into BC blocks away from the CPU, as GpuBlockEncoder does on the GPU: bakes use it for the formats it encodes, the CPU's encoders for the rest.
