Class ernie::TiffImage

Detailed Description

Classic TIFF and BigTIFF, either byte order; strips or tiles of interleaved samples (PlanarConfiguration 1); 8-, 16- and 32-bit unsigned or signed integers and 32-bit floats; uncompressed, LZW or Deflate, with horizontal (2) or floating-point (3) prediction. Of GeoTIFF, the pixel scale, tie point and GDAL's no-data value.

References: * Adobe, "TIFF Revision 6.0", 1992 (the layout, LZW, predictor 2). * Adobe, "Adobe Photoshop TIFF Technical Note 3", 2005 (Deflate, the floating-point predictor). * Aware Systems, "BigTIFF" (64-bit offsets). https://www.awaresystems.be/imaging/tiff/bigtiff.html * OGC, "GeoTIFF Standard" 1.1, 2019 (ModelPixelScale, ModelTiepoint); GDAL's GDAL_NODATA tag (42113).
