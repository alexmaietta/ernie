std::optional< std::filesystem::path > DefaultDirectory ()

Detailed Description

Entries are keyed by a hash of everything they depend on; past capacity bytes the least recently used go (16 GB by default: a global mosaic of Blue Marble's eight tiles is 5 GB of BC7). Any thread may read; failures to read or write are a miss, never an error.
