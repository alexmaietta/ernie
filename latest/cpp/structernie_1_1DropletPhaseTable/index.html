Struct ernie::DropletPhaseTable

ClassList > ernie > DropletPhaseTable

The phase function of a cloud's water droplets in each of a frame's bands, for volumes to read (volume.slang's dropletAt ): Mie scattering by a gamma distribution of droplet radii, its narrow forward peak (withinkDropletForwardDegrees ) folded into the extinction as unscattered light.More...

Detailed Description

The silver lining, the bright sheen opposite the sun on cloud tops, the fogbow near 140 degrees and the glory at 180 come from its lobes. Each band's row, for single scattering, keeps the peak (as Bouthors et al. add it back for light scattered once), over the extinction less its share; the bands' mean, for light scattered many times, has it folded away.

References: * A. Bouthors, F. Neyret and S. Lefebvre, "Real-time realistic illumination and shading of stratiform clouds", Eurographics Workshop on Natural Phenomena 2006 (the droplets' gamma distribution, and the forward peak folded into the extinction, their "modified Mie"). * A. Bouthors, F. Neyret, N. Max, E. Bruneton and C. Crassin, "Interactive multiple anisotropic scattering in clouds", ACM SIGGRAPH Symposium on Interactive 3D Graphics and Games 2008. * J. E. Hansen and L. D. Travis, "Light scattering in planetary atmospheres", Space Science Reviews 16(4):527-610, 1974, section 2.4 (distributions alike in effective radius and variance scatter alike: the gamma's as a log-normal's). https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00168069
