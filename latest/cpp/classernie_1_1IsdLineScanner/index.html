Class ernie::IsdLineScanner

ClassList > ernie > IsdLineScanner

A line scanner (pushbroom camera) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's line scanner model ( UsgsAstroLsSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): line l is exposed around its timeImageTime(l) , a rate's start time plus its seconds per line times (l - its start line + 0.5), through detector line 0.

  • #include <ernie/scene/isd.h>
