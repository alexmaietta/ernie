Struct ernie::CylindricalDem

ClassList > ernie > CylindricalDem

A planet's radius on an equirectangular grid, as LOLA's LDEM products (simple cylindrical, true at the equator) and LROC's NAC DTMs (true at a standard parallel) hold it: heights (metres abovereferenceRadius ; NaN where the product has no data) row by row from the northern, each row from the western sample eastwards,pixelsPerDegree along the meridians and that times cos(standardParallel) along the parallels.More...

Detailed Description

Sample s of line l is centred at latitude maxLatitude - (l + 0.5) / ppd and east longitude westLongitude + (s + 0.5) / (ppd cos(standardParallel)).
