A capped cylinder along its axis.
class GroundPlane
Ernie: an infinite horizontal floor facing +z at a world height, drawn with its material, optionally as a checkerboard.
class Mesh
A polygon mesh: points and the faces over them.
class Plane
A one-sided rectangle facing along its axis.
class PointBased
Geometry from an array of points: a mesh or a point cloud.

Instances of its prototypes (the prototypes relationship's targets) at rows of positions, orientations and scales.

Points drawn as sphereswidths across, coloured by their display colour.

define ERNIE_GEOM_SHAPE_RANGE

Geometry as reflected schemas ( prim_type.h ): UsdGeom's implicit shapes (Cube, Sphere, and along an axis Cylinder, Cone, Capsule and Plane), its point-based Mesh and Points, the PointInstancer and UsdVol's Volume, and Ernie's GroundPlane.

#define ERNIE_GEOM_SHAPE_RANGE `.group = "Shape", .min = 0.0, .max = 1000.0`

Each declaration's fallback is the value Ernie draws an unset attribute with (UsdGeom's own). Bulk arrays (points, topology, instance transforms) are tensors, written with Scene::Write; primvars other than the display colour are an open namespace (primvars:<name>), read by name.

References: * Pixar, UsdGeom, https://openusd.org/release/api/usd_geom_page_front.html * Pixar, UsdVol, https://openusd.org/release/api/usd_vol_page_front.html
