Detailed Description

Without align, each axis keeps the item on the side of at away from the nearer edge: its left (top) edge at at in the image's left (top) half, its right (bottom) in the other, its centre at the centre; so at {1, 0} puts it in the top right corner, and {0, 0.3} hangs it down the left side from there.
