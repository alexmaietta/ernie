Detailed Description

Ernie's own demos derive from it, in C++ and in Python (ernie.demo.Demo), as may any application's.

The scene is the viewer's, or one of the demo's own without a viewer. Give it a camera of its own, even for a demo meant for the viewer (which frames the scene with its own camera): without a viewer, and in tests (RenderImage), the scene renders through it.

In the viewer a demo uses it as any application does (Viewer::AddPanel, OnFrame, SetActiveCamera...); when the Demos panel switches away, what it added is removed (in the demo's scope, Viewer::SetScope) and the viewport's display settings are restored.

References: * Newton's examples (newton.examples: Example, create_parser, init, run and the example browser), https://github.com/newton-physics/newton
