Classes

Type Name
class RenderProduct
One image a render writes: its camera (or a batch of cameras), resolution, render vars and sensor filter chain.
class RenderSettings
A render's settings: its camera, resolution and products, and the rasterizer's options.
class RenderSettingsBase
What render settings and products share (UsdRender's RenderSettingsBase ): the camera, the resolution, and what a product sets in place of its settings' (overlays, minimal shading).

An output of a render product, named by its source, optionally with its own sensor chain.

A SensorFilter prim (Ernie), one step of a sensor filter chain: a filter type and its inputs:* parameters.

Render prims as reflected schemas ( prim_type.h ), following UsdRender:RenderSettings (the rasterizer's options underernie: ) andRenderProduct shareRenderSettingsBase (camera, resolution and what a product may override of its settings), aRenderVar names an output, and Ernie'sSensorFilter is one step of a product's or var's sensor chain.

Each declaration's fallback is the value Ernie renders an unset attribute with, the default of the FrameParams, ShadowSettings or LodSettings field it sets.

The relationships (camera, products, ernie:lights, orderedVars, ernie:filters) are lists of targets; camera is one target, or on a product a batch of cameras. Not declared: a SensorFilter's inputs:*, which its filter type's model (SensorFilterRegistry) defines.
