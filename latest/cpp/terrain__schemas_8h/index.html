Namespaces

Type Name
namespace ernie
Planetary digital elevation models, as the PDS archives them, and the local heightfields Ernie's Terrain draws of them.

Classes

Type Name
class Atmosphere
A planet's atmosphere from a physical model ( EarthAtmosphere ,MarsAtmosphere ): its gases and aerosols scatter and absorb the sun's light on its way to the ground, the sun'sDistantLight then giving the irradiance above the atmosphere.
class StarField
Stars from a catalogue as points of light at infinity, behind everything else, each with a black-body spectrum.
class Terrain
A north-up heightfield (x east, y north, z up) drawn with its bound material, or, with a planet radius, a whole planet of six cube faces ( CubeSphereLod ).
