Detailed Description

Points, normals and colours may be float32 or float64, shaped (n, 3) or flat; indices int32, int64 or uint32. Normals and colours of other sizes (such as face-varying ones) are ignored. Points on a CUDA device come back as zeros of the right number, for the caller to copy device to device. Texture coordinates come per triangle corner from primvars:<set> (constant, uniform, vertex or faceVarying, optionally indexed; see tokens.h), the set being the one the bound material's textures read, else st.

Exception:

  • std::invalid_argument naming the attribute for arrays of the wrong type, size or device, or invalid topology. For a UsdGeom primitive (Cube, Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Capsule, Plane) the generated mesh, PrimitiveMeshOf. A Mesh with a subdivisionScheme is its limit surface instead, refined by OpenSubdiv at SubdivisionLevelOf (Subdivide), its normals the surface's. A skinned Mesh (skel:skeleton) is posed at the stage's time code, before it is refined, unless options say otherwise.
