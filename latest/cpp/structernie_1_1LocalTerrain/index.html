Struct ernie::LocalTerrain

ClassList > ernie > LocalTerrain

A heightfield of a DEM about one point, in a local frame there: x east, y north and z up, its origin on the reference sphere at the point. More...

Detailed Description

Each cell holds the height above that tangent plane of the surface straight below or above it, so the planet's curvature is in the heights (a drop of about x^2 / 2R). Where the DEM has no data the surface is the reference sphere.
