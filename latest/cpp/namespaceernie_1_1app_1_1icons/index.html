Namespace ernie::app::icons

Namespace List > ernie > app > icons

Lucide icon glyphs (lucide-static 1.52.0, private-use codepoints) as UTF-8 strings, for text drawn with the viewer's font, which has the icons merged in: ImGui::Button(icons::kSave) , orstd::string(icons::kCamera) + " Camera" as a label.
