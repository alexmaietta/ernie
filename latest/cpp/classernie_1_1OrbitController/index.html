Detailed Description

It holds no camera of its own: Apply writes its pose, field of view and clipping range into one.

Yaw rotates around the vertical axis (0 looks north, positive turns the view clockwise seen from above); pitch is the elevation angle of the eye above the target's horizontal plane.
