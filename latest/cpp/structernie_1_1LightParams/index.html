Detailed Description

In the rasterizer, distant lights get cascades over a single view, or orthographic maps over the scene in a batch; sphere lights a perspective map when their cone is at most kMaxSpotConeDegrees, else six (a cube); rect and disk lights a cube at their centre.

The rasterizer shades rect and disk lights with their exact Lambertian irradiance, the vector form factor of the emitter clipped to the receiver's horizon (Lambert's polygon formula; Arvo 1994), and the receiver's BRDF evaluated along that vector. Disks are integrated as 16-gons of the same area.

Reference: J. Arvo, "The Irradiance Jacobian for Partially Occluded Polyhedral Sources", SIGGRAPH '94, 343-350, 1994. https://doi.org/10.1145/192161.192250
