Detailed Description

Electrons accumulate as photons times quantum efficiency, plus dark current; the readout adds photon shot noise (Poisson), read noise (Gaussian), and per-pixel fixed patterns PRNU (relative gain) and DSNU (offset); electrons clip at the full well, and the ADC converts them to digital numbers with the ISO-dependent gain, adds the black level and clips to its bit depth. The presets are generic curve shapes, not any particular sensor: replace the channels with measured curves for a specific camera.

Reference: EMVA Standard 1288, "Standard for Characterization of Image Sensors and Cameras", release 4.0, 2021 (the linear camera model).
