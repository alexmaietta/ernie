Struct ernie::PushbroomStrip

ClassList > ernie > PushbroomStrip

A pushbroom camera's strip for one frame ( CameraParams::pushbroom ), as its shaders project with it (projection_pushbroom.slang): its rows' poses run fromfrom (the top row's camera position) toto (the bottom row's), turning fromfromRotation totoRotation along the great arc.More...
