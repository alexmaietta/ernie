Class ernie::RasterSource

ClassList > ernie > RasterSource

A raster image read a rectangle at a time, whatever its file: so that planetary mosaics, elevation models and gigapixel photographs larger than memory are baked, resampled and streamed in parts. More...

  • #include <ernie/util/raster_source.h>

Inherited by the following classes: ernie::MosaicRaster, ernie::PdsImage, ernie::TiffImage
