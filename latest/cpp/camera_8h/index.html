Type Name
struct CameraParams
A camera's parameters resolved from its Camera prim (CameraParamsOf ): a physically based pinhole camera for one image, with UsdGeomCamera's pose, projection and exposure attributes, a spectral range, a lens and a sensor.
struct CameraView
The raster view of a camera for one frame, derived from a CameraParams byCameraParams::RasterView : what the rasterizer's passes need.
struct LensModel
A lens: its projection, natural vignetting and transmission.
struct LensRayTable
A lens given as its rays: unit directions in the camera's frame at every step th sensor pixel corner, (width /step + 1) by (height /step + 1) of them row by row from the top left, interpolated between.
struct Polarizer
A camera's linear polarizer ( tokens::kPolarizerType ): none, one in front of the lens atangle (radians from the image's +x towards +y), or a polarization sensor's mosaic of 2 x 2 pixels at 90, 45 / 135, 0 degrees (Sony's IMX250MZR).
struct SensorChannel
One channel of a sensor: its quantum efficiency (electrons per photon) over wavelength.
struct SensorModel
An image sensor: channels with quantum efficiency curves, full well, noise, gain and ADC.
