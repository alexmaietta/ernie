Struct ernie::GpuXform

ClassList > ernie > GpuXform

A prim posed on the GPU: its transform ops are rows of GPU arrays, one row per prim of one Scene::Write of many paths, so a renderer composes its local transform per instance from the arrays whole, as aPointInstancer 's: translate, then orient, then scale.
