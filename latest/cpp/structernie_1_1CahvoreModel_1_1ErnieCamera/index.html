Struct ernie::CahvoreModel::ErnieCamera

ClassList > ernie > CahvoreModel > ErnieCamera

The model as Ernie renders it, in the model's frame (camera to frame).

  • #include <ernie/scene/cahvore.h>

Public Attributes

Type Name
CameraParams camera
Its pose ( transform ), focal length, apertures and principal point, and the lens: CAHV and CAHVOR as perspective with Brown-Conrady terms, CAHVORE as a Kannala-Brandt fish-eye, each fitted to the model's rays over the image.
double fieldAngleMax = 0.0
The largest field angle over the image, radians.
double pupilShiftMax = 0.0
CAHVORE's largest entrance pupil shift over the image, in the model's units: Ernie's camera is central, so a point at distance d moves by up to about this / d radians.
double residualPixels = 0.0
The fit's largest departure from the model, pixels, over the image: from its distortion's optical axis O lying off the image plane's normal A (a tilt Ernie's lenses, centred on the normal, cannot take; pixels for long focal lengths), and any non-square or skewed pixels.
bool rowsReversed = false
Whether the model's image is a mirror of Ernie's (V running up against H x A): flip the rendered rows.
