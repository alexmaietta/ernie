Detailed Description

Each records what it changes, so Undo puts it back (an attribute's earlier value, or none; a removed subtree with its attributes and tensors) and Redo does it again.

Continuous edits, such as dragging a slider or a gizmo, merge into one step: an open edit of the same attribute as the last step updates that step until Close (called when the drag ends). Edits between BeginGroup and EndGroup, such as defining a prim and setting its attributes, undo and redo together. Edits made to the scene directly are not recorded; undoing past one sets the attribute to what the history last saw.

Reference: E. Gamma, R. Helm, R. Johnson and J. Vlissides, "Design Patterns", the Command pattern, Addison-Wesley, 1994.
