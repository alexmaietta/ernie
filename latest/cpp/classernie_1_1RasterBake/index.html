Detailed Description

A thread of the bake's own reads the raster a part (RasterSource::Parts) and a band of 1024 rows at a time, as RGBA8, and makes the band's mips while they split into whole blocks at its edges, each the 2 x 2 average of the last (in linear light for sRGB colour), exactly the whole image's mips there. The smaller mips come from the bands' last ones joined. Step, on the caller's thread, encodes what is ready (on the GPU with the options' encoder) and writes it in place in the file. At most some 256 MB of band mips wait to be encoded.

Samples are 8- or 16-bit (the high byte kept); one channel is grey, two grey and alpha, three RGB.

References: * R. Geldreich, "bc7enc_rdo" (bc7enc and rgbcx). https://github.com/richgel999/bc7enc_rdo
