Struct ernie::IsdSensor

ClassList > ernie > IsdSensor

A camera's image support data (ISD) in the Community Sensor Model's JSON form, as ALE (the USGS Abstraction Layer for Ephemerides) writes it from SPICE kernels for planetary cameras such as LRO's NAC and WAC, MRO's CTX and HiRISE: the image's size and timing, the focal plane's map to detector pixels, the optics, and the spacecraft's and the body's states over the image. Detailed Description

Lengths are converted to metres; everything else is kept in the ISD's own frames and conventions.

References: * USGS Astrogeology, "ALE: Abstraction Layer for Ephemerides", https://github.com/DOI-USGS/ale * USGS Astrogeology, "usgscsm: Community Sensor Model plugin", https://github.com/DOI-USGS/usgscsm (the sensor models that read these ISDs; its src/Distortion.cpp the lens models). * USGS Astrogeology, ISIS's distortion maps (LroNarrowAngleDistortionMap, ShadowCamDistortionMap, TgoCassisDistortionMap), https://github.com/DOI-USGS/ISIS3 * S. Tulyakov et al., "Geometric calibration of Colour and Stereo Surface Imaging System of ESA's Trace Gas Orbiter", Advances in Space Research 61(1), 2018 (CaSSIS's rational model). * NGA, "Community Sensor Model (CSM) Technical Requirements Document", version 3.0.4.
