Detailed Description

Light polarized along its axis passes transmission, across it transmission over the extinction ratio: a partial polarizer's Mueller matrix, with principal transmittances k1 and k2, turns linear Stokes (I, Q, U), measured from the image's +x, into I' = ((k1 + k2) I + (k1 - k2) (Q cos 2t + U sin 2t)) / 2 at its angle t.

References: * E. Collett, "Field Guide to Polarization", SPIE FG05, 2005 (the Mueller matrix of a linear diattenuator). * Sony Semiconductor Solutions, "Polarization Image Sensor IMX250MZR/MYR", 2018 (the 2 x 2 polarizer layout).
