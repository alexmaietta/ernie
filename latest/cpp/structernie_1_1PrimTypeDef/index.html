Struct ernie::PrimTypeDef

ClassList > ernie > PrimTypeDef

A registered prim type: its name ( Scene::Define 's type), its base, and the attributes it declares itself; abstract ones, such as "Light", are bases only.

  • #include <ernie/scene/prim_type.h>
