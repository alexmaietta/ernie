Class List

Here are the classes, structs, unions and interfaces with brief descriptions:

  • namespace ernie Planetary digital elevation models, as the PDS archives them, and the local heightfields Ernie's Terrain draws of them.
    • class Atmosphere A planet's atmosphere from a physical model ( EarthAtmosphere ,MarsAtmosphere ): its gases and aerosols scatter and absorb the sun's light on its way to the ground, the sun'sDistantLight then giving the irradiance above the atmosphere.
    • struct AtmosphereModel A spherical shell of constituents around a planet of radius bottomRadius , up tobottomRadius + topAltitude .
    • struct AtmosphereParams A planet's atmosphere ( AtmosphereParamsOf ): the physical model's settings and where the planet lies.
    • class AttributeDef One declared attribute of a prim type.
    • struct AttributeMeta How an attribute is described and edited.
    • struct AttributeTraits How the declared C++ type T reads, writes and converts:Read with the fallback (accepting any numeric or vector type an author used, asGetNumber does),Write , andFrom , a value asT or nullopt when it cannot be one.

      • struct Refraction A refracted ray traced from radius r (m) at the apparent zenith cosinemu out of the atmosphere, through its spherically layered refractive index (RefractiveIndex ), by the ray equation d(n t)/ds = grad n with steps of at moststep m.
    • struct BandedConstituent A constituent averaged over a camera's spectral bands.
    • class Boundable What has local bounds: geometry and instancers, with an optional authored extent.
    • class BoundableLight An area light (sphere, rect, disk): a radiant power, or a photometric power or illuminance.
    • struct BrdfModel A reflectance model: shader source plus parameter metadata.
    • struct BrdfParam One scalar parameter of a reflectance model, with what a UI needs to edit it.
    • class BrdfRegistry Reflectance models available to a renderer, keyed by BrdfModel::id .
    • struct CahvoreModel A camera calibrated in JPL's CAHV family of models, as Mars rovers' cameras are (their PDS labels' GEOMETRIC_CAMERA_MODEL, JPL's camera model files): CAHV, a linear (pinhole) model; CAHVOR, with radial distortion about an optical axis; and CAHVORE, with a fish-eye term and an entrance pupil that moves along the optical axis with the field angle, so its rays do not meet at one point.
      • struct ErnieCamera The model as Ernie renders it, in the model's frame (camera to frame).
      • struct Ray A projection ray: its origin (C, or for CAHVORE the entrance pupil's position for its field angle) and unit direction.
    • class Camera A camera looking down its -z axis with +y up: UsdGeomCamera's projection, apertures, clipping, exposure and shutter, and Ernie's lens, scan, spectral sampling, sensor and view poses.
    • struct CameraParams A camera's parameters resolved from its Camera prim (CameraParamsOf ): a physically based pinhole camera for one image, with UsdGeomCamera's pose, projection and exposure attributes, a spectral range, a lens and a sensor.
    • struct CameraView The raster view of a camera for one frame, derived from a CameraParams byCameraParams::RasterView : what the rasterizer's passes need.
    • class Capsule A cylinder with hemispherical caps along its axis.
    • struct Change A prim's changes since a consumer last looked.
    • struct ChangeCursor A consumer's position in a scene's history; see Scene::Changes .
    • class ChunkedFrameImages The images of a frame of a batch of views that do not fit one set of images (such as more views than an image has layers): consecutive chunks of the views, each its own FrameImages .
    • struct ComputeArrayView A Vulkan image seen by a compute API in place, as an opaque array handle (a CUarray for CUDA, ahipArray_t for HIP): read it with that API's texture or surface objects or 2D array copies.
    • class ComputeInterop Zero-copy sharing between a Vulkan device and the compute device behind it (CUDA or HIP), so arrays from compute frameworks (PyTorch, JAX, CuPy, Warp, simulators) reach Ernie's buffers, and Ernie's results reach them, without passing through host memory.
    • class ComputePipeline A compute pipeline over a PipelineLayout .
    • class Cone A capped cone along its axis, its apex at +height / 2.

    • struct Constituent One constituent of an atmosphere: numberDensity particles per m^3 where its profile is 1.
    • class Cube A box centred on its origin, size along each axis.
    • class CubeSphereLod CDLOD terrain over a whole planet: six LodQuadtree height pyramids, one per face of a cube projected onto a sphere of radiusRadius() (a quadrilateralized or "cubed" sphere).
    • struct CubeSphereRegion Region of space holding one node of a CubeSphereLod : every direction withinangle ofaxis , between radiiinner andouter from the body's centre.
    • class CudaInterop ComputeInterop for the CUDA device behind a Vulkan device.
    • class Cylinder A capped cylinder along its axis.
    • class CylinderLight A cylinder along its x axis emitting from its side as a Lambertian emitter, not its caps.
    • struct CylindricalDem A planet's radius on a simple cylindrical (equirectangular) grid, as LOLA's LDEM products hold it: heights (metres abovereferenceRadius ) row by row from the northern, each row from the western sample eastwards,pixelsPerDegree both ways.
    • struct DensityLayer One term of a vertical density profile, relative to the profile's reference density.
    • struct DensityProfile A vertical density profile: the weighted mean of its layers, so 1 at the ground for exponential and double exponential layers.
    • struct DescriptorBinding One binding of a pipeline's push-descriptor set (set 0).

    • struct DetailSettings Tiling of the detail albedo and normal textures.

    • class DistantLight A light at infinity shining along the -z axis of its transform, such as the Sun.
    • struct DomeDirection One of a dome map's regions of about equal energy ( DomeDirectionsOf ), in the map's frame.
    • class DomeLight Light from every direction, optionally from a latitude-longitude map.
    • struct DomeParams An environment light at infinity (UsdLux DomeLight ) resolved for a frame (DomeParamsOf ): radiance from every direction, the spectrumradiance (W m^-2 sr^-1 nm^-1 per camera band) times a relative RGB map whose cosine-convolved spherical harmonics aresh .
    • struct DomeSun A sun found in a dome's map ( ExtractDomeSun ), in the map's frame.
    • struct DomeSunExtraction The sun of a latitude-longitude dome map ( tokens::kLightTexture layout) and the map without it, for lighting the sun as a shadowed distant light while the dome keeps the rest of the sky.
    • struct EarthAtmosphereOptions Earth's atmosphere: an isothermal dry-air column, ozone in a tent profile and a haze aerosol.
    • class EditHistory Undo and redo for scene edits made through it: attribute sets and clears, prims defined, and prims removed with their descendants.
    • struct FrameId Where an image sits in the deterministic random streams of sampling.h .
    • class FrameImages The images a backend renders one frame of a render product into, by render var name: what RenderVarOutput reads, whatever the backend.
      • struct LinearVar A render var the frame holds as one linear buffer over every view rather than an image: written by the frame's own submission, in memory a compute API views in place ( BufferMemory::Shared ), texels offormat view after view.
    • struct FrameParams Per-frame parameters of a render, whatever the backend: the camera, the sun, the other lights, the sensor filters and the frame's place in the random streams.
      • struct SensorChain Several sensor chains over the one raster image, each writing its own vars (render vars with their own ernie:filters ); empty runsfilters forvars .
    • class FrameUploads Host data bound for device buffers, staged for one frame in flight and copied by that frame's own commands ( Record ), so the host can prepare a frame while the device still runs earlier ones that read the same buffers: the copies wait on the device for that earlier work, in queue order, rather than the host waiting for it.
    • struct Frustum Six world-space clip planes of a view-projection matrix, for culling axis-aligned boxes.
    • class Gprim Geometry: drawn in a display colour, or with a bound material.
    • class GpuBuffer A Vulkan buffer with its memory, allocated through VMA.
    • class GpuImage A device-local Vulkan image with a view of all its mips and layers, allocated through VMA.
    • class GpuTimer Times GPU zones ( ERNIE_GPU_ZONE ) for Ernie's profiler (ernie/util/profiler.h ) with timestamp queries: a zone writes one timestamp where it starts and one where it ends in its command buffer, andCollect hands finished zones to the profiler without waiting for the device, then frees their queries by host reset (Vulkan 1.2's hostQueryReset).
    • struct GpuXform A prim posed on the GPU: its transform ops are rows of GPU arrays, one row per prim of one Scene::Write of many paths, so a renderer composes its local transform per instance from the arrays whole, as aPointInstancer 's: translate, then orient, then scale.
    • class GraphicsPipeline A graphics pipeline for dynamic rendering, with dynamic viewport and scissor and no culling.
    • struct GraphicsPipelineDesc Fixed-function state of a graphics pipeline that draws triangle lists without vertex buffers.
    • class GroundPlane Ernie: an infinite horizontal floor facing +z at a world height, drawn with its material, optionally as a checkerboard.
    • struct IesProfile A luminaire's photometric profile from an IES LM-63 file: its luminous intensity in candela over its file's angles ( IesType ), whichSample reads in any direction.
    • struct IesTilt An IES file's tilt data (TILT=INCLUDE, or a tilt file): how its lamp's output changes as the luminaire tilts, for lamps whose output depends on their burning position (metal halide).
    • struct Image RGBA image with linear float channels, rows top to bottom.
    • struct ImageDesc Description of a 2D image or 2D image array.
    • class Imageable What every prim has: visibility and the world it is in.
    • struct InstanceData Per-instance arrays of a point instancer, as UsdGeomPointInstancer defines them.
    • class IsdCameraModel What usgscsm's camera models of image support data share, in the body-fixed frame in metres:
      • struct FrameView A frame of the detector as Ernie renders it: a frame camera of the image's samples by its rows, posed ( camera.transform , camera to body-fixed world) when the frame is exposed, the lens's distortion fitted as Brown-Conrady terms (and scale).
    • class IsdFrame A frame camera from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's frame model ( UsgsAstroFrameSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): the whole image is exposed at once, at the ISD's centre time, from one position and orientation.
    • class IsdLineScanner A line scanner (pushbroom camera) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's line scanner model ( UsgsAstroLsSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): line l is exposed around its timeImageTime(l) , a rate's start time plus its seconds per line times (l - its start line + 0.5), through detector line 0.
      • struct Strip A strip of lines image lines fromfirstLine as Ernie renders it: a pushbroom camera (CameraParams::pushbroom ) whose rows are those lines, posedfrom at the strip's first line's start andto at its end (camera to body-fixed world).
    • class IsdPushFrame A push-frame camera (LROC's WAC, MRO's MARCI) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's push-frame model ( UsgsAstroPushFrameSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): the image is a stack of framelets, each a frame ofFrameletLines lines exposed at once.
      • struct Framelet Framelet __framelet (in image order, from the top) as Ernie renders it: a frame camera of the image's samples byFrameletLines lines, posed when it is exposed (camera to body-fixed world), its exposure the ISD's, the lens's distortion fitted as Brown-Conrady terms (and scale).
    • struct IsdRotation A rotation sampled over time between two NAIF frames, as ALE reads it ( ale::Orientations ): quaternions (w, x, y, z, scalar first) of the time-dependent rotation of vectors from the reference frame into the first oftimeDependentFrames , and a constant rotation (row-major, applied to vectors after it) on into the first ofconstantFrames .
    • struct IsdSamples States sampled over time: ephemeris times (seconds past J2000, TDB) and a value at each.
    • struct IsdSensor A camera's image support data (ISD) in the Community Sensor Model's JSON form, as ALE (the USGS Abstraction Layer for Ephemerides) writes it from SPICE kernels for planetary cameras such as LRO's NAC and WAC, MRO's CTX and HiRISE: the image's size and timing, the focal plane's map to detector pixels, the optics, and the spacecraft's and the body's states over the image.
    • class LaserLight A laser (Ernie): a Gaussian beam of one spectral line along its -z axis, its waist, divergence and power as a datasheet gives them (ISO 11146).
    • struct LensModel A lens: its projection, natural vignetting and transmission.
    • struct LensRayTable A lens given as its rays: unit directions in the camera's frame at every step th sensor pixel corner, (width /step + 1) by (height /step + 1) of them row by row from the top left, interpolated between.
    • class Light Any light: UsdLux's LightAPI inputs, its spectrum's shape and Ernie's physical spectrum.
    • struct LightParams A light besides the sun, resolved for a frame from its prim ( LightParamsOf ).

    • struct LightPatternModel A laser's pattern written in Slang: the relative power a laser sends towards each direction, its watts, beam and shadows staying Ernie's.
    • struct LightPatternParam One parameter of a light pattern, which a LaserLight gives inernie:laser:pattern:params in this order.
    • class LightPatternRegistry Light patterns a renderer can draw, byLightPatternModel::id .
    • struct LocalTerrain A heightfield of a DEM about one point, in a local frame there: x east, y north and z up, its origin on the reference sphere at the point.
    • struct LodPatch One square patch of PatchCells() xPatchCells() grid quads whose vertices are2^level raster cells apart.

    • class OffscreenTarget A colour and depth target for headless rendering, with readback of the colour image.

    • class Overlay A named layer of debug drawing: lines, shapes, markers and labels in world coordinates, drawn over the images of render products that ask for it ( tokens::kOverlays ), after the sensor filters, so they never reach observations that do not.
