Classes

Type Name
class Overlay
A named layer of debug drawing: lines, shapes, markers and labels in world coordinates or posed under prims ( OverlayStyle::anchor ), and text and panels on the image itself (Text ,Rect ), drawn over the images of render products that ask for it (tokens::kOverlays ), after the sensor filters, so they never reach observations that do not.
struct OverlayBatch
Many overlay items of one kind from arrays on the host or a GPU ( Overlay::Batch ), such as one per world of a batched simulation, or a physics engine's bodies, contacts and particles: the renderer reads the arrays as they are, without copies to the host, and draws each item from them on the GPU.
