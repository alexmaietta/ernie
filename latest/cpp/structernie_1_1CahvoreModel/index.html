Struct ernie::CahvoreModel

ClassList > ernie > CahvoreModel

A camera calibrated in JPL's CAHV family of models, as Mars rovers' cameras are (their PDS labels' GEOMETRIC_CAMERA_MODEL, JPL's camera model files): CAHV, a linear (pinhole) model; CAHVOR, with radial distortion about an optical axis; and CAHVORE, with a fish-eye term and an entrance pupil that moves along the optical axis with the field angle, so its rays do not meet at one point. Detailed Description

  • C: the projection centre (for CAHVORE, the entrance pupil at zero field angle), in the model's frame and units (metres for rovers).
  • A: the unit axis perpendicular to the image plane, pointing into the scene.
  • H, V: image x (right) and y (down) of point P are (P - C) . H / (P - C) . A and (P - C) . V / (P - C) . A for a CAHV model: H and V hold the focal lengths in pixels and the image centre.
  • O: the unit optical axis about which the distortion is radial.
  • R: radial distortion coefficients (rho0, rho1, rho2).
  • E: the entrance pupil's movement coefficients (epsilon0 to epsilon2).
  • The CAHVORE type: 1 perspective (linearity 1), 2 fish-eye (linearity 0), 3 general, with its linearity between them. Image coordinates are the models': (0, 0) is the centre of the upper-left pixel, x to the right, y down.

References: * Y. Yakimovsky and R. Cunningham, "A System for Extracting Three-Dimensional Measurements from a Stereo Pair of TV Cameras", Computer Graphics and Image Processing 7(2):195-210, 1978 (CAHV). * D. B. Gennery, "Least-Squares Camera Calibration Including Lens Distortion and Automatic Editing of Calibration Points", in A. Gruen and T. Huang (eds.), Calibration and Orientation of Cameras in Computer Vision, Springer, 2001, 123-136 (CAHVOR). * D. B. Gennery, "Generalized Camera Calibration Including Fish-Eye Lenses", International Journal of Computer Vision 68(3):239-266, 2006 (CAHVORE). * T. Litwin's cmod library (cmod_cahv.c, cmod_cahvor.c, cmod_cahvore.c), as ported by NASA-AMMOS, "CameraModelUtilsJS", https://github.com/NASA-AMMOS/CameraModelUtilsJS (Apache-2.0): ImageToRay follows its cahvore-utilities/cahvore.js. * PDS Imaging Node, "Geometric Camera Models" (MSL GEOMETRIC_CM.TXT), for the conventions. * S. Aiazzi et al., "IRIS: High-fidelity Perception Sensor Modeling for Closed-Loop Planetary Simulations", AIAA SciTech 2022, AIAA 2022-1433 (CAHVORE camera rays in a planetary simulator).
