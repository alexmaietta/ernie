Detailed Description

  • ernieMxPositionWorld(), ernieMxNormalWorld(), ernieMxTangentWorld(), ernieMxBitangentWorld() and their *Object() versions (float3); ernieMxTexcoord(int set) (float2); ernieMxColor(int set) (float4); ernieMxGeomPropFloat(int id), ...Float2, ...Float3, ...Float4 and ...Int, the primvars geomProps names by id; ernieMxViewPosition() (float3), ernieMxTime() and ernieMxFrame() (float).
  • ernieMxLoadFloat(uint offset), ...Int, ...Float2, ...Float3, ...Float4, ...Float3x3 and ...Float4x4: the uniform buffer's values.
  • ernieMxSample(uint slot, float2 uv), ernieMxSampleLevel(uint slot, float2 uv, float lod), ernieMxSampleGrad(uint slot, float2 uv, float2 dx, float2 dy) (float4) and ernieMxTextureSize(uint slot, int level) (int2): the texture table's.
  • ernieMxLightCount() (int); ernieMxLightDirection(int light) (float3, towards it; zero for one that cannot light the pixel); ernieMxLightResponse(int light, float3 response): the surface's response to a unit of the light's radiant intensity, which the shading pass scales by the light's spectrum, distance and shadow.
  • ernieMxEnvironmentRadiance(float3 direction, float alpha) and ernieMxEnvironmentIrradiance(float3 normal) (float3): the light from all around (dome, sky and ambient light), as a GGX lobe of roughness alpha about direction and as cosine-weighted irradiance over pi.

color is what leaves the surface towards the eye, but for the lights' share (reported per light): its emission and environment light. Colours are linear Rec. 709, as MaterialX's are.
