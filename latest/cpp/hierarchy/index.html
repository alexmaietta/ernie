Class Hierarchy

This inheritance list is sorted roughly, but not completely, alphabetically:

  • class ernie::SchemaView Base of the typed views: a handle to one prim of a registered type, read-only when made from a const scene.
    • class ernie::Imageable What every prim has: visibility and the world it is in.
      • class ernie::Atmosphere A planet's atmosphere from a physical model ( EarthAtmosphere ,MarsAtmosphere ): its gases and aerosols scatter and absorb the sun's light on its way to the ground, the sun'sDistantLight then giving the irradiance above the atmosphere.
      • class ernie::BlendShape Offsets of a mesh's points (and normals), weighted by an animation, with inbetween shapes at weights between 0 and 1.
      • class ernie::Boundable What has local bounds: geometry and instancers, with an optional authored extent.
        • class ernie::Gprim Geometry: drawn in a display colour, or with a bound material.
          • class ernie::AxisShape An implicit shape centred on its origin along one of its axes: a cylinder, cone, capsule or plane.
            • class ernie::Capsule A cylinder with hemispherical caps along its axis.
            • class ernie::Cone A capped cone along its axis, its apex at +height / 2.
            • class ernie::Cylinder A capped cylinder along its axis.
            • class ernie::Plane A one-sided rectangle facing along its axis.
          • class ernie::Cube A box centred on its origin, size along each axis.
          • class ernie::PointBased Geometry from an array of points: a mesh or a point cloud.
            • class ernie::Mesh A polygon mesh: points and the faces over them.
            • class ernie::Points Points drawn as sphereswidths across, coloured by their display colour.
          • class ernie::Sphere A sphere centred on its origin.
          • class ernie::Terrain A north-up heightfield (x east, y north, z up) drawn with its bound material, or, with a planet radius, a whole planet of six cube faces ( CubeSphereLod ).
          • class ernie::Volume A participating medium: fields, each named by a relationship field:<name> to a field prim, shaded by the boundMaterial 's volume inputs (density, albedo, emission, temperature).
        • class ernie::PointInstancer Instances of its prototypes (the prototypes relationship's targets) at rows of positions, orientations and scales.
        • class ernie::SkelRoot The root of a skinned character: the meshes and skeletons under it go together.
        • class ernie::Skeleton Joints in a hierarchy, with the transforms their meshes were bound at and their rest pose, posed by an animation source.
      • class ernie::Camera A camera looking down its -z axis with +y up: UsdGeomCamera's projection, apertures, clipping, exposure and shutter, and Ernie's lens, scan, spectral sampling, sensor and view poses.
      • class ernie::FieldBase A field of values over space, which Volumes name; its transform places the field in its Volume (UsdVol'sFieldBase ).
        • class ernie::CloudLayersField Ernie's procedural cloud layers: up to four layers over a repeating weather map, carved from tileable noise at each sample on the GPU (three-geospatial's clouds, after Horizon Zero Dawn's), in the field's frame with z up; a Volume 's density, its material's density scaling it.
        • class ernie::DenseField Ernie's field of a dense array of values, such as a grid solver's smoke or dust each step (Warp's arrays): sampled as a NanoVDB grid of every voxel, which a device array fills in place on the GPU.
        • class ernie::FieldAsset A field read from a file (UsdVol's FieldAsset ).
          • class ernie::OpenVDBAsset A grid of a VDB file.
        • class ernie::NanoVDBField Ernie's field of NanoVDB grids written as an array instead of a file, such as a simulation's each step (Warp's Volume.array() ): one of its grids, read as anOpenVDBAsset 's.
      • class ernie::GroundPlane Ernie: an infinite horizontal floor facing +z at a world height, drawn with its material, optionally as a checkerboard.
      • class ernie::LaserLight A laser (Ernie): a Gaussian beam of one spectral line along its -z axis, its waist, divergence and power as a datasheet gives them (ISO 11146).
      • class ernie::Light Any light: UsdLux's LightAPI inputs, its spectrum's shape and Ernie's physical spectrum.
        • class ernie::BoundableLight An area light (sphere, rect, disk): a radiant power, or a photometric power or illuminance.
          • class ernie::CylinderLight A cylinder along its x axis emitting from its side as a Lambertian emitter, not its caps.
          • class ernie::DiskLight A disk in its xy plane emitting along -z.
          • class ernie::RectLight A rectangle in its xy plane emitting along -z.
          • class ernie::SphereLight A sphere (or point) light, optionally shaped to a cone around its -z axis.
        • class ernie::NonboundableLight A light at infinity (distant, dome), whose photometric brightness is an illuminance.
          • class ernie::DistantLight A light at infinity shining along the -z axis of its transform, such as the Sun.
          • class ernie::DomeLight Light from every direction, optionally from a latitude-longitude map.
      • class ernie::Material How a surface reflects light: Ernie's own inputs, or a UsdPreviewSurface its outputs:surface connects to.
      • class ernie::RenderSettingsBase What render settings and products share (UsdRender's RenderSettingsBase ): the camera, the resolution, and what a product sets in place of its settings' (overlays, minimal shading).
        • class ernie::RenderProduct One image a render writes: its camera (or a batch of cameras), resolution, render vars and sensor filter chain.
        • class ernie::RenderSettings A render's settings: its camera, resolution and products, and the rasterizer's options.
      • class ernie::RenderVar An output of a render product, named by its source, optionally with its own sensor chain.
      • class ernie::Scope A grouping without a transform of its own.
      • class ernie::SensorFilterPrim A SensorFilter prim (Ernie), one step of a sensor filter chain: a filter type and its inputs:* parameters.
      • class ernie::Shader A node of a material's network, of the type info:id names, its inputs constants or connections (<input>.connect ) to other shaders' outputs.
      • class ernie::SkelAnimation Joint transforms and blend shape weights sampled over time.
      • class ernie::StarField Stars from a catalogue as points of light at infinity, behind everything else, each with a black-body spectrum.
      • class ernie::Xform A transform grouping its children.
    • class ernie::Stage A scene's stage metadata, as UsdStage keeps it on its pseudo-root: its units (the up axis, metres and kilograms per unit), its time (time codes and frames per second, the range animation plays over, and the time code it is at) and its default prim.
  • class ernie::AttributeDef One declared attribute of a prim type.
  • class ernie::BindlessHeap Every pass's textures and samplers in one descriptor set (set 1 of every PipelineLayout ), each at an index shaders take from their uniforms: bindless descriptor indexing, so a pass's long-lived textures cost it no binding of its own (bindless.slang declares the arrays).
  • class ernie::BindlessHeap::Slot A slot of the heap, freed when destroyed; move-only.
  • class ernie::BlockEncoder Encodes RGBA8 images into BC blocks away from the CPU, as GpuBlockEncoder does on the GPU: bakes use it for the formats it encodes, the CPU's encoders for the rest.
  • class ernie::BrdfRegistry Reflectance models available to a renderer, keyed by BrdfModel::id .
  • class ernie::CacheKey A cache's 128-bit key for everything an entry depends on: two 64-bit FNV-1a hashes from different starts, each piece length-prefixed.
  • class ernie::FrameImages The images a backend renders one frame of a render product into, by render var name: what RenderVarOutput reads, whatever the backend.
    • class ernie::ChunkedFrameImages The images of a frame of a batch of views that do not fit one set of images (such as more views than an image has layers): consecutive chunks of the views, each its own FrameImages .
  • class ernie::Column One attribute of many prims as rows of one array, as a simulation keeps its bodies' poses: written whole from any array (CPU or CUDA, DLPack, no copy), or mapped and written in place on the device asked for, each write one change ( ColumnChange ) whatever the number of prims.
  • class ernie::ColumnStorage Storage a consumer of columns offers for them on a device it reads them on, such as a renderer's interop buffers: a column mapped there ( Column::Map ) is then written where the consumer reads it, copying nothing.
  • class ernie::ComputeInterop Zero-copy sharing between a Vulkan device and the compute device behind it (CUDA or HIP), so arrays from compute frameworks (PyTorch, JAX, CuPy, Warp, simulators) reach Ernie's buffers, and Ernie's results reach them, without passing through host memory.
    • class ernie::CudaInterop ComputeInterop for the CUDA device behind a Vulkan device.
  • class ernie::ComputePipeline A compute pipeline over a PipelineLayout .
  • class ernie::CubeSphereLod CDLOD terrain over a whole planet: six LodQuadtree height pyramids, one per face of a cube projected onto a sphere of radiusRadius() (a quadrilateralized or "cubed" sphere).
  • class ernie::EditHistory Undo and redo for scene edits made through it: attribute sets and clears, prims defined, and prims removed with their descendants.
  • class ernie::FrameUploads Host data bound for device buffers, staged for one frame in flight and copied by that frame's own commands ( Record ), so the host can prepare a frame while the device still runs earlier ones that read the same buffers: the copies wait on the device for that earlier work, in queue order, rather than the host waiting for it.
  • class ernie::GpuBuffer A Vulkan buffer with its memory, allocated through VMA.
  • class ernie::GpuImage A device-local Vulkan image with a view of all its mips and layers, allocated through VMA.
  • class ernie::GpuTimer Times GPU zones ( ERNIE_GPU_ZONE ) for Ernie's profiler (ernie/util/profiler.h ) with timestamp queries: a zone writes one timestamp where it starts and one where it ends in its command buffer, andCollect hands finished zones to the profiler without waiting for the device, then frees their queries by host reset (Vulkan 1.2's hostQueryReset) some collects later, once their submission has surely completed.
  • class ernie::GradientNoise Perlin's improved gradient noise in three dimensions, its lattice shuffled by a seed, for procedural fields such as volumes' density: smooth, zero at every integer point, and within about [-1, 1].
  • class ernie::GraphicsPipeline A graphics pipeline for dynamic rendering, with dynamic viewport and scissor and no culling.
  • class ernie::IsdCameraModel What usgscsm's camera models of image support data share, in the body-fixed frame in metres:
    • class ernie::IsdFrame A frame camera from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's frame model ( UsgsAstroFrameSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): the whole image is exposed at once, at the ISD's centre time, from one position and orientation.
    • class ernie::IsdLineScanner A line scanner (pushbroom camera) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's line scanner model ( UsgsAstroLsSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): line l is exposed around its timeImageTime(l) , a rate's start time plus its seconds per line times (l - its start line + 0.5), through detector line 0.
    • class ernie::IsdPushFrame A push-frame camera (LROC's WAC, MRO's MARCI) from its image support data, its geometry as usgscsm's push-frame model ( UsgsAstroPushFrameSensorModel ) computes it (IsdCameraModel ): the image is a stack of framelets, each a frame ofFrameletLines lines exposed at once.
  • class ernie::LightPatternRegistry Light patterns a renderer can draw, byLightPatternModel::id .
  • class ernie::LodQuadtree Quadtree over a heightfield for continuous distance-dependent level of detail (CDLOD).
  • class ernie::MappedFile A file mapped read-only into memory: its bytes are read from disk as they are first touched, so a file far larger than memory can be read in parts, the operating system keeping what is used.
  • class ernie::RasterSource A raster image read a rectangle at a time, whatever its file: so that planetary mosaics, elevation models and gigapixel photographs larger than memory are baked, resampled and streamed in parts.
    • class ernie::MosaicRaster One raster of a grid of tiles of one size, type and channels, columns to a row, rows top to bottom: a global mosaic too large for one file (Blue Marble's eight 21600 x 21600 tiles).
    • class ernie::PdsImage A PDS3 image read in place from its mapped file: NASA's Planetary Data System's elevation models and mosaics (LOLA's and MOLA's gridded heights among them), their label attached at the file's start or in a .lbl file beside it.
    • class ernie::TiffImage A TIFF or GeoTIFF image read in place from its mapped file: its layout, and rectangles of its samples decoded on demand, so that images larger than memory (planetary mosaics and elevation models, gigapixel photographs) read a part at a time ( RasterSource ).
  • class ernie::OffscreenTarget A colour and depth target for headless rendering, with readback of the colour image.
  • class ernie::OrbitController Interactive orbit control of a CameraParams around a target point in a z-up world, for inspecting terrain.
  • class ernie::Overlay A named layer of debug drawing: lines, shapes, markers and labels in world coordinates or posed under prims ( OverlayStyle::anchor ), and text and panels on the image itself (Text ,Rect ), drawn over the images of render products that ask for it (tokens::kOverlays ), after the sensor filters, so they never reach observations that do not.
  • class ernie::TileStore Where a virtual texture's resident tiles live on the device: a sparse image's bound tiles ( SparseImage , filtered in hardware) or a pool of pages (PagePool , paged in software).
    • class ernie::PagePool The software path of virtual textures, for devices without sparse residency and textures larger than the device's images: resident tiles live in pages, the layers of one 2D array image, which shaders find through a page table ( VirtualTexture ) and filter themselves (bilinear within a page, trilinear across two mips).
    • class ernie::SparseImage A 2D image whose memory is bound tile by tile (Vulkan sparse residency), the hardware path of virtual textures: a texture far larger than device memory holds only the tiles in view, and filtering spans tiles in hardware.
  • class ernie::Path Absolute path naming a prim in a Scene , as in USD:/ is the root and/World/Sun is the childSun of/World .
  • class ernie::PipelineLayout A pipeline layout with one push-descriptor set (set 0), the device's bindless heap (set 1, BindlessHeap ) and an optional push-constant block shared by all stages.
  • class ernie::Prim Handle to a prim in a Scene : its path plus the scene, for chained edits.
  • class ernie::PrimSet A fixed list of prims to write rows of one tensor to at once ( Scene::Write ), such as a simulation's bodies every step: the scene looks the prims up the first time and keeps them, looking them up again only after prims are removed or for another scene.
  • class ernie::RasterBake A colour or data raster baked into a DDS file a band of rows at a time, as BakeTexture would bake it whole: for images larger than memory, than JPEG's 65535 limit or than one file (aMosaicRaster ), and to spread a bake over a viewer's frames.
  • class ernie::RefractiveIndex A complex refractive index tabulated at increasing wavelengths (nm), linearly interpolated and constant beyond its ends.
  • class ernie::RenderDelegate A render backend for a Scene , after Hydra's HdRenderDelegate: the rasterizer, and in future ray and path tracers, behind one interface that applications, the GUI viewer and the Python bindings drive without knowing which backend renders.
  • class ernie::RenderVarOutput One render var of one rendered frame, such as LdrColor , named after UsdRender'sRenderVar .
  • class ernie::Renderer Ernie's front door for rendering a Scene headless: a GPU and a render backend on it, with no graphics API to set up.
  • class ernie::Sampler An owned VkSampler .
  • class ernie::Scene A lightweight, USD-shaped scene for Ernie: a tree of typed prims named by Path , each holding attributes by USD name, plus change tracking for render delegates.
  • class ernie::ScopedGpuZone An ERNIE_GPU_ZONE for Ernie's profiler: timestamps around the rest of the scope's commands.
  • class ernie::Selection The prims of a type that hold some attributes ( Scene::Select ), as a Fabric query finds the buckets of a type and attributes: found again only once prims of the type come, go, or author or clear an attribute, and kept in onePrimSet meanwhile, so the columns of its attributes stay theirs from step to step.
  • class ernie::SensorFilterRegistry Custom sensor filters a renderer can run, by SensorFilterModel::id .
  • class ernie::ShaderCompiler Compiles Slang modules at run time.
  • class ernie::Spectrum A spectrum tabulated at increasing wavelengths and linearly interpolated between them; constant beyond the first and last samples.
  • class ernie::StepOperation A submitted step: poll or wait for its GPU work, then fetch its outputs.
  • class ernie::Swapchain A presentation swapchain for a window surface, with one "rendered" semaphore per image.
  • class ernie::Targets Relationship targets given to a setter: one target (a Path or its text), stored as a singlePath , or a list of them, stored as a list; reads take either.
  • class ernie::TemperatureModelRegistry Temperature models a renderer can run, by TemperatureModel::id .
  • class ernie::Tensor A strided n-dimensional view of typed memory with an optional owner that keeps it alive: Ernie's end of DLPack.
  • class ernie::TextureCache Textures prepared at load kept between runs as DDS files (as RasterDelegate keeps image files' textures encoded to BC7 on the GPU), so that a texture decodes, mips and encodes once until its file, or how it is read, changes.
  • class ernie::Value A scene attribute value: one of the scalar, vector, matrix, token, relationship and small-array types Ernie's schemas use.
  • class ernie::VulkanContext Owns a Vulkan instance, device, graphics and compute queue, and memory allocator.
  • class ernie::WorkerPool A few threads running jobs in the order they come, such as textures decoded while frames render.
  • class ernie::XformPart A part of XformOps , set or not, read asstd::optional<T> is (if (part) ,*part ).
  • class ernie::app::Demo A demo: a scene built in the constructor, advanced by Step , drawn byRender and checked byTestFinal , run byDemoRunner in a window, headless or without a viewer.
  • class ernie::app::DemoRunner Runs demos as Newton's examples run: opens the viewer the options ask for (or none), runs a demo until its window closes or it has run its frames, then writes --output , runs its final test and reports the benchmark.
  • class ernie::app::FileDialog A modal file browser drawn with Dear ImGui, which Viewer shows forOpenFileDialog : a folder's contents with an Up button, an editable path and shortcuts to home, the working directory, the root and the folders used recently, a name field, a file type menu and Open (or Save) and Cancel.
  • class ernie::app::Panel A dockable window of the viewer.
    • class ernie::app::FunctionPanel A panel that draws its contents by calling a function.
    • class ernie::app::WidgetPanel A panel of simple widgets described by data rather than ImGui calls, so code without ImGui bindings (Python) can build one.
  • class ernie::app::Timeline The animation's clock, as Omniverse Kit's timeline: plays the scene's time code ( Stage::GetTimeCode ) over the stage's range, from its start time code to its end, in real time or a frame at a time, looping or stopping at the end.
  • class ernie::app::Viewer An interactive window onto an Ernie Scene : a docked viewport rendering the scene with aRenderDelegate (the rasterizer), plusStage , Property, Render Settings, Sensor, Stats and Log panels.
  • class ernie::app::ViewerLogs What the viewer's logging calls collect ( Viewer::LogScalar ,LogArray ,LogImage ), after Newton's viewers'log_scalar ,log_array andlog_image : scalar signals and arrays drawn in the Plots panel, and images each in a panel of its own, as textures the GPU samples where they were written.
  • class ernie::profiler::ScopedZone Times the rest of the enclosing scope as a CPU zone named name while recording.
  • struct ernie::AnimationPose A SkelAnimation 's samples interpolated at a time: empty arrays for those it lacks.
  • struct ernie::AtmosphereModel A spherical shell of constituents around a planet of radius bottomRadius , up tobottomRadius + topAltitude .
  • struct ernie::AtmosphereParams A planet's atmosphere ( AtmosphereParamsOf ): the physical model's settings and where the planet lies.

  • struct ernie::BandedConstituent A constituent averaged over a camera's spectral bands.
  • struct ernie::BrdfModel A reflectance model: shader source plus parameter metadata.
  • struct ernie::BrdfParam One scalar parameter of a reflectance model, with what a UI needs to edit it.
  • struct ernie::CahvoreModel A camera calibrated in JPL's CAHV family of models, as Mars rovers' cameras are (their PDS labels' GEOMETRIC_CAMERA_MODEL, JPL's camera model files): CAHV, a linear (pinhole) model; CAHVOR, with radial distortion about an optical axis; and CAHVORE, with a fish-eye term and an entrance pupil that moves along the optical axis with the field angle, so its rays do not meet at one point.
  • struct ernie::CahvoreModel::ErnieCamera The model as Ernie renders it, in the model's frame (camera to frame).

  • struct ernie::CameraParams A camera's parameters resolved from its Camera prim (CameraParamsOf ): a physically based pinhole camera for one image, with UsdGeomCamera's pose, projection and exposure attributes, a spectral range, a lens and a sensor.

  • struct ernie::ColorPrimvar The colours a material's diffuse colour reads through a UsdPrimvarReader_float or _float3: the mesh primvar ( primvars:<name> ), one value or three each (width ), and the reader's fallback where a mesh has none.

  • struct ernie::DomeParams An environment light at infinity (UsdLux DomeLight ) resolved for a frame (DomeParamsOf ): radiance from every direction, the spectrumradiance (W m^-2 sr^-1 nm^-1 per camera band) times a relative RGB map whose cosine-convolved spherical harmonics aresh .

  • struct ernie::EarthAtmosphereOptions Earth's atmosphere: an isothermal dry-air column, ozone in a tent profile and a haze aerosol.
