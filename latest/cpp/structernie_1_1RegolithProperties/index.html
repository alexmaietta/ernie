Struct ernie::RegolithProperties

ClassList > ernie > RegolithProperties

A regolith's thermophysical properties and its body's sunlight, the Moon's by default: density rising from its surface value to its deep one over an e-folding depth, contact conductivity following the density, radiative conductivity growing with the cube of temperature, and heat capacity a quartic in temperature.
