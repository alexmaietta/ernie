Struct ernie::CloudNoise

ClassList > ernie > CloudNoise

The tileable noise textures procedural cloud layers are carved from (volume.slang's cloudLayersDensity ): a Perlin-Worley shape and a Worley detail, each repeating in all three axes, a local weather map of the layers' coverage and a curl-noise turbulence, both repeating across.More...

Detailed Description

Values over 0 to 1, x fastest.

References: * A. Schneider and N. Vos, "The Real-Time Volumetric Cloudscapes of Horizon: Zero Dawn", SIGGRAPH 2015 Advances in Real-Time Rendering (Perlin-Worley shapes eroded by Worley details). * S. Hillaire, TileableVolumeNoise, 2017, MIT (https://github.com/sebh/TileableVolumeNoise): the tileable Worley noise and the textures' recipes. * takram, three-geospatial's clouds (packages/clouds/src/shaders: cloudShape, cloudShapeDetail, localWeather and turbulence), 2024, MIT, Copyright (c) 2024 Shota Matsuda: the frequencies and weights here.
