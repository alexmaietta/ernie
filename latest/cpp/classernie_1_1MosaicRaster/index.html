Class ernie::MosaicRaster

ClassList > ernie > MosaicRaster

One raster of a grid of tiles of one size, type and channels, columns to a row, rows top to bottom: a global mosaic too large for one file (Blue Marble's eight 21600 x 21600 tiles).More...

Detailed Description

Its parts are its tiles, read (decoded) one at a time; its grid, its first tile's spread over the rest.
