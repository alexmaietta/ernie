Struct ernie::VolumeSettings

ClassList > ernie > VolumeSettings

How finely volumes render ( RenderSettings 'ernie:volumes:* ): steps through each volume, towards each light, up and down for the sky's light (0, unoccluded) and through the air in front (0, none), the most orders of multiple scattering, and the texels across the volumes' shadow maps.

  • #include <ernie/session/frame_params.h>
